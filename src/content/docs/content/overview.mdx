## About this guide
This editorial style guide defines how the New Jersey Innovation Authority writes for the people of New Jersey. It covers voice, tone, grammar, punctuation, formatting, and inclusive language. It outlines everything a content designer, writer, or editor needs to produce clear, consistent, human-centered content across NJ digital products and services.

This guide is based on the [AP style guide](https://www.apstylebook.com/), [Federal Plain Language Standards](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language), and the [NJIA Accessibility Enablement Playbook](https://github.com/newjersey/enablement/blob/main/accessibility/playbooks/accessibility-enablement.md).

### Translated and multilingual content
This guide covers English-language content. Capitalization, punctuation, and tone conventions vary across languages and do not translate directly. 

### Project-specific guidance
Some projects may need to adapt these guidelines. In those cases, create a project-specific style guide that references this document as its foundation and notes only the places where it diverges. Don't start from scratch. Document the exceptions, like changes due to audience, platform, or other partner requirements.
