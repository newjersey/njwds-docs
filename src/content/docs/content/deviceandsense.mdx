Use language that works regardless of how a user accesses the content: screen, screen reader, phone, or other device.

## Interaction language

Use "select" instead of "click," "tap," or "press." These terms assume a specific device.

### Do
- Select submit 

### Don't 
- Click the submit button
- Tap submit
- Press submit 


## Sensory language

Avoid "view," "see," "hear," and "listen" – they assume a specific ability or device. Use intent-based language instead.

### Do
- Go to your medical records
- Review your lab results
- Check your claim status

### Don't 
- View your medical records
- See your lab results


## Directional language

Avoid references to screen position – layout shifts across devices and screen sizes.

### Do
- Select the link from the main menu

### Don't 
- Select the link on the top right corner of the screen


## Color references

Do not use color to give instructions because it may confuse users who have colorblindness.

### Do
- Select submit

### Don't 
- Select the blue Submit button
