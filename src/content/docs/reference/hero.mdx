## Usage Guidelines

### Use this component for:
<IconList>
    <IconListItem icon="check">
        **Prominent information** you'd like to call out at the top of a website.
    </IconListItem>
</IconList>

### Use something else for:
<IconList>
    <IconListItem icon="close">
        **Any content below the fold.**
    </IconListItem>
</IconList>

## Content guidelines
- **Keep heading length to 6 to 10 words.** Bring attention to a project priority.
- **Keep body text to 10 to 30 words.** Support the callout with some short explanatary text. You don't need more than a couple of sentences.
- **Choose a meaningful CTA** This will be the main action the whole page points towards. Ensure you are leading users to the most meaningful next step.
